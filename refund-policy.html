No returns on digital products

Every product on this site is a digital file, delivered by email. Because the file can be downloaded and used as soon as it's delivered, we are not able to offer returns, exchanges or refunds once a product has been sent — in the same way a physical store couldn't resell an opened item.

If something goes wrong

"No refunds" doesn't mean we'll leave you without a working product. If any of the following happens, contact us and we'll sort it out at no extra cost:

  • You paid but never received your files.
  • The zip file is corrupted, incomplete, or won't open.
  • You typed your email address incorrectly at checkout and need it resent to the right address.
  • The product doesn't match its description on this site.

In these cases we'll redeliver, fix, or otherwise make it right. We just aren't able to offer a refund simply because you changed your mind after receiving the files.

Before you buy

Read each product's description carefully, and contact us first if you're not sure a product is right for you. We're glad to answer questions before you pay.

How to request help

Email charlessikha1@gmail.com, and include your order ID from your payment receipt so we can find your purchase quickly.
